# Recruitment — standards and compliance design assessment (20 November 2026)

**Status:** Standards assessment supporting the **owner-approved conceptual STD-A through STD-F requirements** (accepted 20 November 2026); individual external standards, their editions and adoption tiers remain subject to applicability assessment. No normative governing amendment, legal opinion, ISO certification or operational acceptance is asserted. [Recruitment domain record](../docs/capabilities/recruitment/capability-record.md) | [Draft PR #99](https://github.com/nuxt-layers/architecture-blueprint-review/pull/99).

## Scope and normative hierarchy

Accepted ADRs and governing platform requirements take precedence within this blueprint. The external matrix distinguishes **applicable statute/regulation** (binding only under established jurisdiction, dates, activity, employer and data controller role), **international standards** (voluntary unless contracted/adopted), **interoperability specifications** and **reference guidance**. Technical compliance to ISO/WCAG/OWASP does not certify lawful hiring. Record edition, applicability and assessment/evidence status; never blindly apply a UK-only restriction worldwide.

**2026 UK automated decision correction:** DUAA changed UK GDPR automated significant-decision provisions. Do not encode an outdated categorical prohibition based on former Article 22. Treat **human review by default** as a stricter *product safety design choice*, not universal law. Legal Compliance must load current permitted grounds, special-category constraints and safeguards (including information, contest and intervention/reconsideration where applicable) for each deployment. A controller cannot bypass applicable obligations by labelling a rubber-stamp as human review.

## Proposed operational design derived from the standards

**Policy loading:** A domain command obtains tenant/employer and role from authenticated caller, resolves resource owner and legal jurisdiction, applicable effective-time legal constraints from Legal Compliance, privacy/retention floors from Privacy and employer-configured business preferences from Configuration. Authorisation makes resource-level decisions; Recruitment evaluates the native business guard and records policy references, evidence revision and effective time. On unavailable statutory/funding/access prerequisites, return indeterminate/blocked rather than approve. No arbitrary boolean overrides of legal floors.

**Workflow:** Maintain separate requisition, approved recruitment opening, campaign/adverts, application, assessment, selection, offer and handover state machines. A request is accepted only after access, policy, source validity and optimistic-revision validation; perform native atomic decision + domain outbox. Workflow/Scheduler coordinate timed work but do not own domain state. Provider responses remain pending/confirmed/failed/unknown; an ACK is not proof of publication or contract execution.

**Sensitive decisions:** Require job-related documented criteria, interview rubric and attributable qualified reviewers, conflict/recusal evidence and separate accommodations. Define assessment validity and comparability, human/AI provenance, review and appeal flags. Human involvement by default; jurisdictionally permitted automation is optional after explicit legal/risk gate.

**Retention:** Each evidence class has purpose, legal basis, controller, provenance, sensitivity, creation/effective/deletion eligibility, legal hold and per-destination disclosures. Expiry triggers assessed retention workflow, not irreversible blind deletion; candidate pool reuse demands independently validated purpose and sharing permission.

**Exposure:** Public adverts expose only approved fields and structured-data mappings; internal views enforce field-level restrictions; applicant views avoid confidential panel notes; Reporting receives lawfully minimised correction-aware facts; Audit receives consequential event evidence; Logging receives redacted operational signals. No universal candidate dossier or provider SDK type in public contract.

**Acceptance dimensions:** native no-provider operation; no unapproved opening; accessible alternative candidate intake; stale/contradictory permissions/qualification denial or review; UK legal-policy effective-date transitions; no cross-employer applicant leakage; per-channel uncertain publication; offer handover timeout/retry; retention/hold collision; discriminatory ranking challenge; restricted data exports; versioned API compatibility, idempotent replay and source-outbox duplicate handling.

## Further standards adoption and detailed design considerations

1. Adopt ISO 30405:2023 and ISO 10667-1/-2:2020 as core recruitment/assessment design references, keeping formal certification out of scope.
2. Require **jurisdiction-aware compliance profiles** across employers, agencies, roles, stages and effective dates, rather than embedding UK rules globally.
3. Mandate structured, job-related decision evidence and human review by default, while keeping legally permitted automated significant decisions behind a separately authorised policy gate.
4. Adopt HR Open Standards/schema.org as **adapter/mapping profiles**, never canonical Recruitment storage models.
5. Require a traceability matrix from each accepted standard/regulatory control → Recruitment/public port owner → configuration/guard → evidence → conceptual acceptance scenario; avoid burdening native recruitment with optional provider or AI infrastructure.

**Next stage after fuller specification sign-off:** separately research permissive-licence OSS packages/versions, dependency graph and maintenance/security/licence provenance, rather than choosing packages before domain boundaries stabilise.

## Supplementary standards and legal controls identified during review

- **[ISO 30400:2022](https://www.iso.org/standard/78044.html) HR vocabulary:** Align definitions in the public conceptual contract, without changing governing catalogue names or making ISO terminology a persistence schema.
- **[SIOP/APA Principles for the Validation and Use of Personnel Selection Procedures, fifth edition (2018)](https://www.apa.org/ed/accreditation/personnel-selection-procedures.pdf):** Strong professional reference for relevance/validity, reliability and fair interpretation of structured interviews, tests and scoring. Use for evidence of method suitability, qualified use and reevaluation. Complement ISO 10667; not a legal certification.
- **[EU Directive 2023/970](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2023/970/oj/eng) on pay transparency:** For employers subject to implementing national law, support accessible early pay/pay-range disclosure, gender-neutral job adverts and a rule disallowing salary-history questions. Jurisdiction, national transposition/effective dates and employee thresholds need Legal Compliance verification; do not treat a directive as identical nationwide runtime rules.
- **[EU GDPR Article 22](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32016R0679)** remains distinct from amended UK UK-GDPR automated decision rules. Store jurisdiction/authority and rule version per decision; do not use a single global `automated_decision_allowed` flag.
- **[NIST SP 800-218 SSDF](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/218/final):** Supporting secure software delivery guidance for the later implementation and dependency review, not a recruitment-owned business process.

**Priority refinement:** include an explicit vacancy pay-disclosure source/approval field, a validation/relevance evidence reference for every scored assessment, and a legal-jurisdiction-specific automated-decision policy outcome in the next accepted domain contract revision.
