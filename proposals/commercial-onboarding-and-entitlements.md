# Platform commercial onboarding and tenant service entitlements

**Status:** Blueprint conceptual proposal, not implemented or an accepted governing change. **Baselines:** `architecture-rules/master` `2eea557fa3acbf9412fea065d2a3cdda42a764fc`, blueprint `master` `442b3018c580562cac7cb513dbee9e7ad5d9a707`. See [tenancy/governance](organisational-tenancy-and-governance.md), [policy boundaries](policy-and-governance-boundaries.md).

## Model and approved discussion direction

A basic human personal account and personal group are free and self-service. An organisation/tenant creation request is self-service **but is commercially gated** according to selected paid services and host policy. Service selection is modular: each available capability may offer independently selectable tier/tariff; optional bundles, added seats, storage and discounts; changes later. Payments, subscription rights, tenant provisioning, feature activation and person permissions are distinct authoritative results. The project owner broadly endorsed these directions; the COM decisions below are **proposed conceptual elaborations**, not accepted APIs or implementation completion.

The platform operator is a real business with its own organisational tenant, commercial catalogue, payment recipient and accounting records. The installation/host is not itself a tenant. Operator financial scope is isolated from tenants' own invoices/payment processing. **Bill-to entity may differ from service-consuming tenant** (e.g. parent purchasing for subsidiaries); parentage never automatically establishes financial obligation.

## Ownership and contract allocation

| Owner | Source facts / business decisions | Inbound → outbound and absence |
|---|---|---|
| Authentication / Identity | Free person registration, personal group; authorised tenant/root group and human governance | Verified subject/host-controlled provisioning → opaque identifiers/created/held outcomes; operator-only root provisioning remains current implementation |
| Configuration / archetype | Allowed feature selections and tenant/group settings with version/provenance | Approved setting/catalogue → effective scope; no permissions or commercial entitlements generated by configuration |
| Product / Pricing | Marketable service catalogue, tiers, valid price/discount/quote, coupon conditions and redemption reservation/commit rules (promotion owner boundary open) | Eligibility, context, coupon → versioned quote; invalid/expired/exhausted rejected; coupon not consumed on failed payment |
| Order | Accepted purchase/amendment and legal/commercial commitment | Confirmed quote and purchaser/bill-to authority → accepted/pending/rejected order; no payment or feature activation assertion |
| Subscription | Plans, renewal, cancellation, suspension, entitlement scope and effective dates | Accepted order/verified payment conditions → effective feature/seat/storage entitlements; cannot grant Identity memberships or domain permissions |
| Billing / Invoice / Accounting | Charge calculation/cycle/proration; issuance; ledger respectively | Versioned use and entitlement evidence → assessed charge, invoice/posting results independently; never conflate finalisation and settlement |
| Payment Gateway | Verified authorisation/capture/refund and provider reconciliation | Authorised payment instruction / provider evidence → pending/settled/failed/unknown; browser success is never final confirmation |
| Host onboarding composition / Workflow | Onboarding orchestration/progress, retries, checkpoints, compensation | Owned result references → provisional, provisioning, ready, partial, refund-review, cancelled; no reownership of Identity or financial state |
| Authorisation | Scope, resource permission, step-up, independent grants and approvals | Verified caller/resource/domain policy → access decision; subscription entitlement alone cannot authorise individual |
| Storage and other resource owners | Tenant/group scoped file operations and usage; service-specific actual activation | Effective quota/entitlement and authorised operation → operational/denied/pending; no commercial decision in Storage |
| Audit / Logging / Reporting / Notifications | Evidential trail / sanitised diagnostics / derived analytics / authorised notices | Scoped domain outcomes, failures and policy versions → own purpose-limited results |

## Conceptual onboarding sequence

1. Free personal signup and verified identity; no implied entitlement to operate paid services.
2. Provisional organisation request: name, type, jurisdiction, proposed human owner/real-world authority, desired new tenant or authorised existing/child tenant. Do not expose privileged Identity migrations to HTTP.
3. Select checkbox services and independently priced tier per capability; optional bundle, licensed seats and media/files storage quota.
4. Pricing validates versioned tariff, coupon, maximum redemption/eligibility and applicable commercial policy. Display currency, periods, taxes, recurring and one-off costs, refund/cancellation terms.
5. Order records confirmed commercial commitment and bill-to/recipient roles; payment or approved credit arrangement follows; provider evidence is verified independently.
6. Subscription establishes commercially effective entitlements; host orchestration invokes authorised Identity provisioning and configuration, service enablement and resource quotas. Track independent statuses: **paid/entitled/provisioned/enabled/authorised**.
7. Activate dashboard only on operational confirmation; users with access may invite workforce, order new services and amend tiers. On failed provisioning after settled payment, reconcile, resume or refund under policy; never charge again or falsely display readiness.

## Public conceptual surfaces and invariants

| Input | Result | Validation / failure semantics |
|---|---|---|
| Proposed tenant group, controller evidence, originator | Provisional onboarding request | Server verifies actor/approval/policy; duplicate operation identifier returns prior request |
| Capability/plan/tier/quantity/storage/coupon choice | Versioned priced quote | Validate dependency, region, support, capacity, price and coupon scope; expiry/reprice explicit |
| Approved order, payer, verified payment outcome | Subscription start/renew/upgrade/downgrade/suspension | Invoice, payment and entitlement results remain distinct; out-of-order, repeated or unknown provider responses reconciled |
| Effective feature and member-seat entitlement | Activated feature and permitted seat budget | Identity retains membership authority; define included seats, contractors, pending invites, customer exclusion, deduplication across groups and changes after suspension |
| Effective storage GB/objects allowance | Quota enforcement/operational result | Current Storage contract reports usage but **does not enforce allowance**. Add guarded admission for concurrent or pending uploads; provider bucket creation per tenant not required |
| Cancellation, grace, delinquency, over-quota | Restricted/read-only/recovered service state | No immediate arbitrary deletion; distinguish retention/legal holds, export, grace and billing obligations |

**Storage baseline:** `nuxt-layers/storage/CONTRACT.md` uses environment-level S3 buckets and keys scoped by tenant/group; `usage()` is informational, explicitly not a limit. Hetzner S3 is for application images/files only, never the platform DB, Temporal or other infrastructure backups. Purchased capacity is a logical quota, not preallocated bucket bytes. Identify the owner of quota reservations/admission and concurrency before implementing.

**Commercial protections:** no trust in user-supplied price, tenant claim or payment redirect; no entitlement as permission; no provider callback assumed unique; no cross-tenant commercial leakage; high-risk subscription payer changes independently authorised; parent administration and bill-to scope distinct; event sourcing by authoritative owners, at-least-once delivery with dedupe; effective date/versioned proration and coupon state; isolated platform-operator business financial records.

## Host GUI

Free account → Create organisation → select legal/organisation archetype → choose services (checkbox), independent tier cards, member seats and storage → coupon → review recurring and one-off costs and terms → payment → progress/status → capability dashboard. Later: subscription management, permitted upgrades/downgrades, seat/usage monitor, invoices, payment method, grace warnings, export and cancellation. Host owns navigation, all actions server-authorised, headless and presentation separate, accessibility/i18n mandatory. Mark services pending instead of assuming availability.

## Scenarios

- Free member of drama club incurs no personal organisation provisioning fee; any club subscription belongs to a separately authorised payer.
- Guest house purchases Booking Standard and extra staff seats; receptionist receives only assigned permissions, guests not silently billed as workforce seats.
- Company pays for a child tenant; losing parent administration does not erase child organisation records or change historic bill-to authority.
- Valid first-purchase coupon reserves against checkout, consumed only at confirmed commercial commitment; retry/failure not double redeem.
- Payment settles but S3 quota activation unavailable: remain pending, retry/reconcile and remedy; no fictitious quota increase.
- Tenant exceeds downgraded quota: preserve existing files subject to policy, restrict new writes and provide remedial path; no arbitrary deletion.
- Cancellation with legal hold preserves covered records, not unfettered future paid operations.
