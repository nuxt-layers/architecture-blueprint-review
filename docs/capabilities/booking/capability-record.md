## Cross-capability note — Asset availability and reservation boundary (15 November 2026)

**Status: Proposed relationship only; Booking's conceptual review remains incomplete.** [Asset](../asset/capability-record.md) owns physical/entitlement restrictions, applicable usage eligibility, assignment and condition. Booking may consume authorised asset availability and request a checked allocation or release, while Booking remains authoritative for reservation, cancellation and time-slot rules. A reservation does not bypass an Asset safety/legal/licence restriction, and an Asset assignment must not create a duplicate Booking reservation. Design a correlated concurrency-safe confirmation/reconciliation protocol; Booking's absence cannot disable native Asset management.
