## Cross-capability note — Billing counterparty and contract authority (15 November 2026)

**Proposed reciprocal relationship; Business is still an unreviewed candidate, not a confirmed canonical contract or counterparty owner.** [Billing](../billing/capability-record.md#cross-capability-completeness-review--further-interfaces-and-relationships-15-november-2026) requires verified references to legal commercial counterparties, bill-to/recipient/payer roles, effective agreement and permitted invoice snapshots, even when the customer is not an Identity member. Business, Company, Organisation Model or another approved owner must eventually supply the authoritative party/contract references; this note does **not** decide which owns them. Billing returns authorised charge/exception status and does not maintain the party master, contracts, bank details or login identity. Ambiguous party authority blocks affected charge/invoice handoff; cross-tenant and related-company contracts need explicit permission. Resolve ownership in relevant later reviews rather than creating a Billing-owned business directory.
