# **Capability Record**

## Capability Name:

`assessment` — Assessment definitions, assessment execution and authoritative learner assessment results.

## Status: (Available, Installed, Configured, Operational, Enabled, Authorised and Proposed).

**Proposed — owner-requested candidate record (17 November 2026).** Not Available, Installed, Configured, Operational, Enabled or Authorised. No implementation repository, released contract, API or runtime is asserted. This is the **pro-forma for a later independent conceptual review**, not a completed capability review.

## Purpose

Own the reusable assessment instruments and the lifecycle of conducting and evaluating individual assessments. Course defines intended learning outcomes and which assessment requirements must be met; Assessment defines assessment instruments and records the individual attempts, submissions, marking, moderation, appeal and reassessment. Training independently determines verified achievements and course completion from authorised assessment evidence and other participation facts.

## Classification

**Domain candidate**. Independent domain records/lifecycle justify a dedicated future assessment review. Implementation architecture, package, storage and GUI remain undecided.

## Dependencies

**Potential contractual cooperation (not approved package dependencies):**
- [Course](../course/capability-record.md): versioned learning outcomes, required assessment references, pass/completion specifications. Course does not own learner attempts or marks.
- [Training](../training/capability-record.md): consumes authoritative, provenance-rich assessment results and determines competency/completion; must not overwrite Assessment marks.
- [Enrolment](../enrolment/capability-record.md): identifies relevant learner participation/eligibility and applicable course version, but does not assign a mark.
- Identity, Authentication, Authorisation, Profile, Privacy and Legal Compliance: verified and permission-scoped learner/assessor relationships, assurance, safeguarding, retention and restrictions. Missing mandatory policy/permission data fails closed.
- Publisher, Content Security, Media/Storage: permitted assessment content, submitted files, malware/safety gates and versioned references where applicable.
- Workflow, Clock, Audit, Logging, Reporting: moderated marking and appeal choreography, deadlines, evidence, diagnostics and reporting without reowning assessment data.

All build/runtime dependency edges must be explicit, directional and acyclic; cooperation may use public queries, host-provided ports or committed events.

## Optional integrations

External examination platforms, proctoring, marking services, approved examination boards and remote question banks through provider-neutral [Integrations](../integrations/capability-record.md). Provider results require identity, provenance, integrity, authorised scope, version and status checks before acceptance. A remote provider is not automatically an awarding authority.

## Capabilities offered

**Proposed native responsibilities for review:**
1. Define versioned examination/assignment/practical assessment instruments: questions/tasks, instructions, mark schemes/rubrics, pass thresholds, outcome mappings and permitted accommodations, subject to licensing and permitted reuse.
2. Schedule/authorise an assessment attempt *in cooperation with* cohort/session and Enrolment authorities; own attempt eligibility outcome only when explicitly delegated via a trusted public decision, never infer a seat or enrolled status.
3. Accept and validate submissions/observations securely, with integrity, provenance, permitted late-submission and permitted-assistance rules.
4. Support examiner marking, second marking/moderation, review, appeal, adjustment and reassessment as explicit state transitions with evidence and separation of duties.
5. Publish authorised results, including pass/fail or score, effective rubric version, confidence/verification state, corrections, invalidation and appeals; supply results to Training and other authorised consumers.
6. Preserve lawful histories, provider statuses and idempotent/event-driven reconciliation; mark unknown, provisional, disputed, withdrawn and final results distinctly.

**Explicit exclusions:** Course curricula; learner admissions/cohort allocations; attendance or final course completion; institutional recognition/accreditation; *formal qualification awards*; credential issuance, verification, replacement or revocation; self-declared assessment success as a credential.

### Boundary, authority and essential native lifecycle

Assessment is a **Domain candidate** justified by reusable versioned instruments and an individual assessment case lifecycle that exists without a Course or final award. It owns: (a) item-bank and instrument definitions, versions, rubrics, mark schemes, assessment-specific outcome mappings and release policies; (b) assessment sittings/authorised attempt references, submissions, observations, evidence integrity and invigilation/proctoring *assessments*; (c) original marks, reviewer/second-marker and moderation decisions, adjustments, assessment appeals and reassessments; (d) assessment result status and publication, corrections and historically attributable invalidations. Question content and marking IP require licence/usage controls. Publisher/Media/Storage own their source artefacts/bytes; Assessment owns versioned assessment semantics and the linked evidence manifest, integrity fingerprints and admissibility decisions, not a duplicate content repository.

Assessment does **not** own Course outcomes/completion rules; admission, individual allocation, cohort standing or venue bookings (Enrolment/Events/Booking); learner participation, competence verification or course completion (Training); qualification specification, equivalence/award authority, qualification appeal or withdrawal (Qualification); credential issuance/status (Credentials); IAM, legal authority, raw files, generic workflow or audit logs. A passed exam is **neither a verified course completion nor a qualification award**.

**Proposed state model:** instrument `draft → validated → authorised/published → superseded/retired` (withdrawal may restrict new attempts while retaining historical version); attempt `planned → eligible/authorised → active → submitted/observed → under review → moderated → determined → releasable/released`, with explicit `cancelled`, `void`, `disputed/appealed`, `corrected`, `reassessment linked` and `on hold` transitions. Result finality, publication and underlying decision are **separate attributes**: internally determined is not automatically disclosed; an appeal can suspend downstream reliance without destroying its predecessor. Missing or conflicting mandatory information gives `blocked/unknown`, not success; timeouts create an explicit pending/uncertain state only where preserving a quarantined, non-executable evidence receipt is necessary and safe. No unscanned attachment becomes executable, browsable or eligible for grading.

**Proposed invariants:** never rewrite a published assessment instrument's historical interpretation; no marking against an unpinned rubric; one accepted decision revision for a given assessment attempt at a time, with correction chain; no assessor marks an attempt in conflict with configured separation-of-duties rules; no submission/results access from tenant ancestry or a learner identifier alone; no provider result becomes final without authenticated provenance, admissibility and explicit authorised acceptance. All transitions capture actor/decision authority, why, effective time, recorded time and prior revision.

### Cooperation, direction, provider boundary and failure

- **Course ↔ Assessment (conditional):** Course supplies required outcomes, specification, permissible rubric criteria; Assessment supplies versioned reusable instruments and individual evidence/results. Course never mutates marks and Assessment never declares course completed. Independent exam requires no Course package.
- **Enrolment ↔ Assessment (conditional):** Enrolment supplies verified standing/attempt-entitlement and course revision; Assessment supplies attempt/result status for permitted learner coordination. A stale enrolment decision blocks dependent starts; results remain historically attributable after enrolment withdrawal.
- **Training ← Assessment results (conditional):** Training consumes final or explicitly policy-eligible grading evidence plus later invalidation/revision; Training owns competency and course-completion decisions and must re-evaluate reliance on changed marks. Assessment does not call Training's private stores.
- **Qualification ← Assessment results (conditional):** Qualification consumes individual assessment evidence and revisions for eligibility/award adjudication, including direct candidates; qualification-level appeal or changed award is Qualification-owned. A corrected mark triggers review/notice, **not** automatic new award or revocation.
- **Credentials:** no direct exam-pass-as-qualification credential grant. Any credential attesting an assessment-only claim requires an independently approved claim issuer and source contract; Credentials owns issuance/verification/status, never grades.
- **Publisher/Media/Storage/Content Security:** authoring and secure evidence references are distinct from versioned marking state. Signed URLs or storage possession do not confer access to marks; avoid putting sensitive item banks or answers in general-purpose public search indexes.
- **Identity/Authentication/Authorisation/Profile/Verification:** identity and session claims, examiner mandates, permissions and accommodations are independently validated; none of these cross-owner inputs alone constitutes assessment success.
- **Workflow/Scheduler/Clock/Notifications:** orchestration, reminders and time calculations do not grade; transitions must be independently authorised and idempotent; missing notices do not reverse committed results.
- **Integrations:** external exam boards, proctors or marking providers are optional provider-neutral adapters. Native authoring, manual grading and appeals operate without them. Externally authoritative outcomes retain external source ownership, explicit verification, expiry and conflict states; retraction is not inferred from a timeout.
- **Audit/Logging/Observability/Reporting/Privacy/Legal Compliance:** Assessment owns assessment-change evidence and lawful retention execution; Audit records independent accountability, Logging excludes answer data/secrets, Reporting produces authorised aggregates, Privacy and legal owners supply constraints. All resource access is purpose-limited.
- **Dependency discipline:** reciprocal information flows are not mutual package imports. Composition injects public ports/adapters or subscribes to durable events in a single acyclic implementation graph; absence modes are per interface, not guessed from a Nuxt layer's presence.

### Trust authority and cross-institution evidence (ASMT-14)

- **Verification** owns qualified issuer/provider identity, authenticity, evidence and issuer/delegation trust assessment for specific claims, purposes, jurisdictions, dates and relying parties. Trust combines identity, cryptographic integrity, issuer mandate/accreditation evidence where applicable, subject binding, claim type, permitted scope, proof method/assurance, freshness and expiry/revocation. Return explicit qualified outcomes, policy/version, basis and caveats, not a universal provider `trusted=true` or numeric score.
- **Legal Compliance / authoritative external bodies** retain legal/accreditation facts; Verification reports source-qualified evidence, not invented statutory recognition. **Federation** verifies/coordinates remote peer transmission but trust in a peer or Hub is not transitive issuer acceptance. **Integrations** owns provider discovery, transport, authenticated callbacks and credential references; successful connectivity does not establish educational authority. Identity/Authentication/Authorisation provide principal, session and permitted access, not provider trust.
- **Assessment** defines the assessment-specific local acceptance policy and decides whether verified external grading evidence is admissible for a specified instrument/attempt/outcome/purpose (including marking standard, result validity, moderation, exam equivalence when necessary and rights to receive/reuse). A provider might be valid for one examination and unacceptable for another. **Training** decides competence/completion; **Qualification** decides qualification award/credit equivalence and qualifications-specific recognising-body authority. Neither accepts a Verification result as an automatic grade, achievement or award.
- Local policy may use risk-specific threshold categories (unverified identity, identity-only, verified scope, enhanced assurance), but the labels do not imply universally portable rank or legal status. Each decision must retain exact qualified claim/issuer/scope, policy revision, evidence references, verifier, evaluation time, maximum staleness and reason. `unknown`/provider outage is not `revoked` and cannot be `accepted` where fresh assurance is mandatory.
- Acceptance path: source result and consent/permission → authenticated Integrations/Federation transport or direct evidence → Verification qualified trust/status → Assessment assessment-specific admissibility and historical capture → Training/Qualification independent outcome decision → Credentials only on independently authorised claim/award. On trust downgrade, source correction, key compromise or expiry, notify/recheck affected evidence dependencies without silently undoing previously authoritative decisions.

| **IN** Submission and evidence intake | Attempt, answer/observation, admissible attachments and screening, integrity proof, submission time, accommodation/late policy and lawful disclosure | Accepted receipt vs quarantined/pending/rejected evidence and protected evidence reference (Assessment); source bytes remain Publisher/Media/Storage-owned. |
| **IN** Mark, moderation and finality commands | Authorised marker(s), assessment/rubric revision, criterion marks, reasons, examiner conflict/identity controls, expected revision | Original and moderated mark decision, appealability, release/finality and correction lineage (Assessment). |
| **IN** Appeal, incident, correction and reassessment | Subject/appellant standing, grounds, referenced decision revision, adjudicator authority and supporting evidence | Appeal case, explicit stay/admissibility, outcome, correction/new revision or rejection; original history preserved (Assessment). |
| **IN** External import and trust/reconciliation | Provenance-rich external result, verified source/issuer evidence, lawful permitted reuse, Verification trust verdict+freshness, approval scope, prior status | Assessment acceptance/rejection/held assessment-evidence reference, with trust provenance and continued-reliance status; never a formal Qualification award. |
| **IN** Effective IAM, policy, provider and time context | Identity/Authentication/Authorisation/Configuration/Privacy/Legal Compliance/Clock/Verification/Integrations as relevant | Protected action blocked on missing mandatory permissions/assurance or required fresh provider trust; explicit unsupported/unavailable/indeterminate rather than silently succeeding. |
| **OUT** Instrument and rubric read model | Authorised instrument/criterion revision, release and permitted audience | Assessment-owned published description/rubric reference, not leakable confidential question content and not Course curriculum. |
| **OUT** Attempt, submission and marking views | Authenticated learner/examiner/authorised administration context, tenant/resource scope | Revisioned status, eligible evidence receipt, marking queue, moderation, confidential or released result according to release policy (Assessment). |
| **OUT** Assessment result and dependency status | Subject/attempt/instrument/rubric IDs, finality/release, appeal hold, evidence provenance and effective times | Authoritative assessment result snapshot; downstream admissibility indicator for Training/Qualification, never automatic competence/course completion/award. |
| **OUT** External evidence trust and acceptance status | Provider source, Verification evaluated trust claim, scope, staleness, licence/disclosure and local acceptance policy revision | Verifiable as-of trust provenance and Assessment's own accepted/held/rejected grade-evidence decision, not a universal institution accreditation. |
| **OUT** Scoped export and historical changes | Authorised learner/institution/relying party, export purpose, privacy/legal rules | Minimised historical/result evidence, revision chain, disclosure and rights constraints, export status (Assessment). |

| **OUT** Audit, Logging and Reporting signals | Material actor/decision/access events, sanitised error IDs, approved reporting scope | Audit independently records who/what/when/authority; Logging operational failures without leaking scripts; Reporting derived pass rates, marking throughput, moderation, appeals, integrity/quality metrics; no re-ownership of results. |

**Common published semantics:** Public operations distinguish commands, queries, committed change events and actual disclosure; all carry contract version, scoped resource/owner references, applicable policy/version, identity/assurance, source/evidence provenance and freshness where necessary. Write operations use expected revisions and retry-safe idempotency; query results mark restricted, unknown and stale states; actor/tenant scope and purpose are enforced server-side. Stable error concepts: unauthorised, forbidden, wrong tenant, invalid instrument/attempt state, invalid revision, expired/invalid evidence, ineligible, unsupported assessment, source unknown, provider unavailable, trust insufficient, disclosure prohibited, content unsafe and conflict/retry pending. No invented exactly-once guarantee.

**Cross-owner correction guarantee:** Correction, appeal hold, external trust revocation or supersession produces attributable Assessment state revision and committed event. Training and Qualification re-evaluate their *own* dependent decisions; Credentials relies on its actual authorised source, and absence of event receipt requires reconciliation by querying revisioned status. Historic completed decisions are not silently erased.

**Additional conceptual acceptance cases:** valid signed examination record from previously unknown provider → identity verified but scope unapproved → held/rejected for verified reliance; known recognised provider with expired mandate → blocked pending fresh evidence; two relying institutions with different acceptance policies → independent, potentially different decisions on same verified result; valid source + candidate refused lawful sharing → no disclosure; source revokes result after it was accepted → preserve history and issue recheck/invalidated-dependency event, with independently controlled Training/Qualification effects; replayed external event → one accepted revision.

**Latest governing reconciliation (18 November 2026):** ADR-0017 and ADR-0018 are now accepted. Assessment must document and enforce tenant/resource-authorised access without inherited parent-tenant privilege; retain attributable human governance and protect changes to approval/trust policies; distinguish legal duties and mandatory invariants from configurable assessment acceptance thresholds. Verification supplies qualified facts; Assessment owns and enforces its own local admissibility policy, under independently authorised policy approval. No new domain owner or automatic transitive trust follows.

## Owner approval — external assessment trust and public contract (18 November 2026)

**Status: Owner-approved conceptual architecture, not an implemented or released contract.** The owner approved the Assessment/Verification trust split, independent relying-party decisions, the complete idealised public inbound/outbound contract inventory, command/query/event model and recommendations recorded above. This clarifies the already approved ASMT-06–ASMT-14 rather than opening a new domain.

**Accepted responsibility allocation:** Verification evaluates issuer and evidence trust for a specified identity, claim, purpose, jurisdiction, scope, assessment type, relying party and effective period; Integrations/Federation convey authenticated evidence but confer no trust. Assessment alone decides whether that verified evidence is acceptable as an assessment result for the relevant instrument, grading rules and institution. Training independently decides competency/course completion; Qualification decides qualification relevance, recognition and award; Credentials represents only properly authorised source outcomes. No trust transitivity, universal numeric trust score, new trust capability or unauthorised award.

**Required Verification-to-Assessment public input/output interface:** Conceptual `EvaluateIssuerTrust` (subject to the Verification owner contract) receives issuer/provider identity, result/evidence proof, requested claim and assessment type, intended purpose, relying institution, jurisdiction, effective time, required assurance and applicable policy version. It returns the scoped qualified trust decision (**sufficient / insufficient / rejected / indeterminate**, not a universal provider approval), authenticated issuer/subject and claim binding, verifiable source authority/mandate where supported, permitted scope, evidence and cryptographic provenance, method/assurance, verification time, expiry/revocation freshness, reasons and limits. Missing mandatory evidence or stale/unknown status holds or rejects protected acceptance; a provider outage must not be described as revocation.

**Required Assessment external-evidence acceptance interface:** Conceptual `EvaluateExternalAssessmentEvidence` receives the result reference and immutable source version, relevant instrument/rubric/requirement and desired reuse, the qualified Verification response, lawful disclosure/transfer and licence evidence, tenant/relying institution context and its own approved acceptance-policy revision. It returns **accepted / held / rejected / requires review**, the locally authoritative admissibility decision and rationale, result revision, source and trust provenance, policy/effective timestamps, recheck conditions and downstream reliance restrictions. It is not a Qualification award or a credential. Controlled historical exports and committed correction/trust-invalidation events support later re-evaluation; no automatic revocation of downstream outcomes.

**Contract publication requirement:** When an implementation exists, its independently usable root `CONTRACT.md` must publish the complete Assessment **inputs** (instrument governance, enrolment/standalone eligibility, attempt/response intake, grading/moderation, appeals/corrections, external result imports and trust evidence, IAM/policy/time) and **outputs** (instrument revisions, receipts, attempt status, result/finality and appeals, admissibility/trust provenance, historical exports, committed events and sanitised Audit/Logging/Reporting signals). Each surface must state source owner, authorised consumer, revision/version, validity/freshness, permission and purpose, failure/unknown behaviour and retry/idempotency for commands. Public contracts are independent of database tables and provider SDKs.

**Approval limitations:** This approves the business contract boundaries and operating semantics, **not** a final endpoint signature, universal trust-tier taxonomy, an issuer registry, provider certification scheme, legal acceptance or implemented interface. Those remain Verification/Assessment contract alignment and acceptance tasks. Keep the existing draft PR until the mandatory latest-master technical and architectural checks and separate explicit merge approval.
