## Cross-capability note — Subscription versus Billing (15 November 2026)

**Proposed; BIL-02 remains open.** [Subscription](../subscription/capability-record.md) owns plan/entitlement, approved subscription start/end, pause, renewal and cancellation state. [Billing](../billing/capability-record.md#idealised-public-inbound-contract) consumes versioned subscription facts and owns period assessment, rating, prorating, billing-run results and charge state. Billing supplies charge/status references but cannot grant or revoke entitlements; Subscription does not assert invoice issuance or settlement. Disputed/out-of-order changes require explicit effective-date policy and reconciliation. No implementation dependency has been authorised.
