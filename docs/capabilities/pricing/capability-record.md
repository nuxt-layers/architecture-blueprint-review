## Cross-capability note — Pricing and Billing authority (15 November 2026)

**Agreed conceptual relationship: BIL-01 approved on 15 November 2026; implementation remains unverified.** [Pricing](../pricing/capability-record.md) owns the commercial offer, tariff, quoted price and relevant rate revision. [Billing](../billing/capability-record.md) consumes attributable/effective prices and evidence to decide and explain the charge for a specific period, quantity and agreement; it does not rewrite an authoritative tariff. Missing/stale/contradictory price evidence holds the charge. Billing can supply usage of price revisions for reconciliation without transferring price ownership.
