## Cross-capability note — Billing legal entity and tenant boundary (15 November 2026)

**Proposed reciprocal interface, not a completed Tenancy review.** [Billing](../billing/capability-record.md#cross-capability-completeness-review--further-interfaces-and-relationships-15-november-2026) requires trusted tenant isolation, while charge issuer legal entity, accounting books, customer/supplier contract party, bill-to and payer may all differ. A tenant or Identity group is not automatically the legal issuer or debtor. Billing receives permitted tenant/entity association and independently verified party/contract authority; it supplies charge status and scoped commercial references, not a tenancy or corporate registry. Cross-tenant/entity charges, queries and exports require explicit authority; missing required legal-party attribution holds the affected action. No direct import cycle or implemented contract is implied.
