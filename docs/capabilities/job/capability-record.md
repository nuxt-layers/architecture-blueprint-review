## Employment reciprocal conceptual interface — 17 November 2026

**Status: Proposed in [Employment PR #59](https://github.com/nuxt-layers/architecture-blueprint-review/pull/59), not a Job review approval or implemented contract.** Job should own the definition and lifecycle of positions/vacancies; Employment owns person-to-employer engagements, effective terms and assignment facts. Employment may consume valid Job/position references and report dated assignment/occupancy signals; Job must not infer employment, contract acceptance or Identity membership from a position reference. Missing Job does not necessarily block an engagement without a post. Public contract direction and absence/failure rules must be resolved in Job's dedicated review.

## Qualification trust and employment interoperability — reciprocal proposal (18 November 2026)

**Status: QUAL-06–08 owner-agreed; QUAL-09 accepted in principle, details proposed in [Qualification PR #65](../qualification/capability-record.md).** Job/position owner should define versioned essential/desirable educational and professional qualification requirements and accepted alternatives for particular roles, not award or verify qualifications. Qualification supplies source-qualified awards, scheme/level evidence and narrowly scoped comparison to Job/employer-supplied requirements. `evaluateQualificationRequirements` must not decide hiring, establish right to practise, or silently turn an unverified/foreign award into locally recognised standing. Recruiting process makes final human/legal suitability decisions; missing evidence is inconclusive, not automatically disqualifying. Exact Job interfaces remain for Job review. This note does not assert a published API or independent approval for job.


## Legal Compliance reciprocal conceptual contract — 19 November 2026 (draft PR #85)

**Status: proposed, pending owner approval; not an implemented or published interface.** [Legal Compliance dedicated conceptual contract](../legal_compliance/capability-record.md#legal-compliance--dedicated-conceptual-review-19-november-2026) proposes scoped, time/version/source-qualified legal applicability, mandatory restrictions, holds, deadlines and uncertainty. **job** supplies only authoritative, minimally necessary resource/service/activity/tenant context and completion/refusal evidence through documented public contracts; Legal Compliance does not take ownership of job's records, business decisions or enforcement. Statutory interpretation is not personal Authorisation, Privacy's specialist consent/rights rules, or proof of regulator acceptance. Missing or conflicting mandatory legal qualification holds only the affected unsafe action; authorised source owners report applied decision version and partial/failed enforcement. Cross-capability cooperation is host-composed, not an implicit direct package dependency. Existing neighbouring decisions and active PR proposals retain their own status.
