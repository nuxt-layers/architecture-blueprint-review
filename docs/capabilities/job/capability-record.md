## Employment reciprocal conceptual interface — 17 November 2026

**Status: Proposed in [Employment PR #59](https://github.com/nuxt-layers/architecture-blueprint-review/pull/59), not a Job review approval or implemented contract.** Job should own the definition and lifecycle of positions/vacancies; Employment owns person-to-employer engagements, effective terms and assignment facts. Employment may consume valid Job/position references and report dated assignment/occupancy signals; Job must not infer employment, contract acceptance or Identity membership from a position reference. Missing Job does not necessarily block an engagement without a post. Public contract direction and absence/failure rules must be resolved in Job's dedicated review.
