## Cross-capability review note — Enrolment (provisional)

**Enrolment reciprocal interface (proposed; 17 November 2026; [Enrolment review PR #60](https://github.com/nuxt-layers/architecture-blueprint-review/pull/60)).** Training owns independently verified attendance, competencies, completion, qualifications and evidence; Enrolment owns admission and participation status. Training may consume effective enrolment as prerequisite context and supply verified prior-learning/completion facts to Enrolment. Neither event automatically reassigns the other's domain status absent explicit course/programme rules. This is not Training review approval or a verified implementation.
