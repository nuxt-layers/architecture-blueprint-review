## Cross-capability note — Blog article editing (15 November 2026)

**Proposed reciprocal relationship; independent Content Editor contract not verified.** [Blog](../blog/capability-record.md) may use Content Editor's reusable formatting controls to edit host-registered Publisher article types through Publisher's documented editor slot, type schema, safe rich-text profiles and save/transition interfaces. Content Editor owns presentation behaviour only: it must not store article versions, make publishing decisions, bypass Publisher's validations or assume a direct private import. The host may replace the editor; source authorisation remains server-side. This note does not complete Content Editor's conceptual review.
