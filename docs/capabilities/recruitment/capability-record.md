## Purpose and scope

Provide a coherent, employer- and jurisdiction-scoped recruitment function from authorised demand or lawful speculative intake to evidence-backed selection, conditional offers and independent Employment handover. Preserve distinct source authorities and records for each candidate, vacancy, campaign and decision. Support a host-composed headless core and optional accessible Nuxt presentation. A native implementation must not require Job, Publisher, external ATS, boards, AI/ML, Workflow or Notifications merely to record a legitimate application and selection.

**Classification:** Domain candidate, concept approved. **Intended contract:** provider-neutral, versioned commands, scoped queries and committed Recruitment-owned events (`R-IN-01`–`R-IN-10`, `R-OUT-01`–`R-OUT-09`). These are business semantic identifiers, not deployed endpoints or a published wire protocol. ### Independently meaningful responsibility, records and exclusions

**Owner-approved classification: Domain capability.** Recruitment is the employer/authorised recruiting organisation's bounded, policy-governed selection and vacancy management domain; it is not a generic job-definition editor, a workforce establishment ledger or a general-purpose workflow engine.

Recruitment **owns**: requisition case and explicit approved opening allocation (authority source and capacity only to extent approved); recruiting campaign and per-channel publication intent/status evidence; sourcing lead and applicant submissions; recruitment-specific candidate dossier and purpose-restricted evidence references; candidacy/application relationship, stage history and evaluation decisions; interview/assessment requests and recorded recruitment evaluations; conflicts, recusals, review and appeals; selection and pre-engagement offer/acceptance negotiation record as an **approved conceptual boundary**; handover request/acknowledgement and reconciliation; recruitment-specific withdrawal, retention holds and defensible history. Records carry employer/tenant, jurisdiction/purpose, revision, source, actor/decision mandate, effective/recorded times and disclosure classification.

**Not owned:** Job position definition; authorised finance budget, payroll, establishment-wide workforce capacity, Employment's actual contracts/engagements/assignments; Identity principals, Profile's canonical personal data; Qualification awards or Verification attestations; assessment instrument's source grades; provider job-board listing as a remote fact; Publisher document artefacts; delivery of messages; workflow/task/scheduler engines; Audit evidence ledger; Reporting aggregates. A candidate record must not silently become a global person/profile identity; an application must never produce an Identity membership or Employment engagement automatically.

**Distinct objects.** A position describes reusable work; an approved requisition represents permission to recruit for a stated purpose, employer and bounded number of openings; an opening allocation is one authorised recruitment capacity unit (not necessarily a formal establishment seat); a campaign can market several openings and a single opening can have several channel adverts. A candidate may submit multiple separately governed applications. A selection decision is not an offer; an offered arrangement is not accepted; acceptance is not an active contract or actual occupancy. Retain independent states and identifiers.

**Operating modes.** Native/manual mode supports authorised internal requisitions, direct applications, manually recorded interviews, private selection, correspondence records and manual Employment handover without Job, Publisher, external ATS, boards or automated workflow. Applications without a vacancy/position are expressly designated speculative or pooled, with separate lawful purpose, expiry and no promise of an opening. An unauthorised requisition cannot be published or represented as approved. Optional delegated/agency, internal-only and external-ATS modes must preserve the owner of each fact, source status and migration/reconciliation history; withdrawal of a provider must not erase legally retained decisions.

### Native state machine and recovery

- **Requisition:** draft → awaiting delegated approval → approved/rejected/cancelled; approved → open/suspended/expired/closed, optionally reopened by a fresh documented authorisation. An expired or revoked authorisation prevents new allocations/publication, without erasing past applications.
- **Opening:** authorised available → reserved for proposed selection → committed to handover → independently confirmed engagement or released/reopened/expired. Reservation does not imply paid headcount, budget approval or employment.
- **Campaign/adverts:** draft → approved disclosure → publication requested → acknowledged/confirmed/rejected/unknown; confirmed → revision requested/withdrawal requested → confirmed withdrawn/unknown. Track each channel separately; no optimistic public visibility assertion on ACK.
- **Application:** received → eligibility/completeness triage → under review → interview/assessment/selection → selected or rejected/withdrawn/closed. Correction, challenge/appeal, restricted hold and re-evaluation are attributable transitions, not erasures.
- **Offer:** drafted → authorised → presented → candidate accepted/declined/expired/withdrawn; contingencies pending/satisfied/failed/unknown; handover requested → Employment acknowledged/rejected/unknown → engagement confirmed, where independently evidenced. Do not infer right-to-work or joining from acceptance.
- **Failures:** duplicate submissions return the original result when key and intent match; contradictory replay rejects; concurrent revisions conflict; source truth disagreement records a disputed state and reconciliation action; timeouts mark unknown/pending, never success; privileged overrides retain reason, approval and audit reference.

**Commands**, conceptually: initiate/revise/approve/cancel requisition; allocate/release/reopen an opening; draft/approve/publish/withdraw a campaign; receive/correct/withdraw application; record evidence, evaluation, conflicts or human selection; issue/revise/withdraw/accept an offer; request/reconcile Employment handover; request/resolve appeal; perform retention/disclosure actions. Server validates actor, employer, mandate, policy, revision, permitted transitions and idempotency. Asynchronous completion has explicit accepted/pending/completed/failed/indeterminate results.

**Queries:** position-linked openings; vacancy/campaign status by channel; authorised applicant/case/pipeline/history; permissible evidence completeness and provenance; offer/appeal/handover state; policy/retention exposure. All reads are tenant- and role-filtered, explicit about stale or incomplete external evidence, with as-of/effective/recorded time and version. Search indexes are derived projections, not recruitment authority.

**Events:** emitted only after durable domain transaction via Recruitment-owned outbox (ADR-0012). Message Queue transports, replays and may duplicate; consumers de-duplicate and independently authorise any subsequent command. A received Recruitment event cannot create an Employment contract, publish a Publisher document, grant access, verify qualification or authorise payment. No distributed exactly-once claim.

**Shared context and invariants:** per-operation employer/tenant and scoped candidate/application/opening reference, actor/agency delegation and session assurance where material, access decision, source/evidence revision, legal/policy/retention version, reason and decision mandate, effective/observed/committed time via Clock, correlation and idempotency where appropriate. These fields are proportionate per operation; **no universal request object**. Stronger controls govern bulk export, protected characteristics, disciplinary data, exceptional overrides and selection decisions. Never overwrite historical decisions on later policy change; recompute eligibility as a new attributable determination. A missing upstream fact remains unknown, not false or approved.

Required collaboration is defined by **public contracts/host composition**, not mandatory import edges. Maintain acyclic dependencies and independent failure semantics. Recipients record corrections or revocations instead of treating old events as eternal truth.

### Legal, security, privacy and provider neutrality

Apply governing OWASP ASVS 5.0 Level 2 with Level 3 safeguards where heightened risk warrants, WCAG 2.2 AA, BCP 47/CLDR, tenant isolation and least privilege. Recruitment handles unusually sensitive candidate information; use purpose-limited collection, encryption/access segregation, retention and deletion schedules, evidence provenance, incident response and restricted bulk exports. Treat special-category and protected-characteristic information as separately controlled; do not make irrelevant protected data visible to assessors or use it in ranking. Accessible alternate application routes, reasonable adjustments and human review/appeal are required where relevant; automated scoring cannot silently deliver solely automated consequential decisions without applicable lawful assessment. Bias monitoring uses lawful minimised measures, not an uncontrolled global candidate profile.

Legal Compliance must identify country/service-specific employment, equality, anti-discrimination, right-to-work, background-check, agency, record-retention, immigration and automated decision requirements; **this proposal is not legal advice or compliance certification**. Candidate notices, withdrawal, correction, subject-access and appeals must identify actual owner and lawful basis; consent is not the default lawful basis for all recruiting processing. Cross-border transfers and agency sharing demand independent permissions and documented purpose. A later legal hold can suspend erasure without making old evidence generally readable.

Native mode uses internal case records and manual publication/communication. Optional provider connectors disclose configured mappings, credential ownership, webhook authenticity, delivery limits, costs, retention/access and cross-border implications. With an external ATS as preferred operational system, explicitly designate local authoritative decisions versus mirrored external facts; import/replay, reconciliation, exit and portability cannot convert provider acknowledgements into local approvals. Preserve limited readable export when a provider is lost, subject to legal restrictions.

### Conceptual GUI, administrator and headless requirements

Host-owned navigation follows **sidebar → Recruitment dashboard**, with role-aware workspaces: requisitions/opening authorisation and funding reference, campaigns and channel publication reconciliation, candidate intake and privacy notices, redacted application pipeline, interview/assessment scheduling, evaluator conflict declarations, guarded selection/offer action with explicit authority, Employment handover and unknown/retry queue, appeals/correction/retention oversight, and restricted operator diagnostics. An optional candidate portal shows submitted applications, document requests, candidate-visible stages and lawful rights; **never** internal evaluation or other candidate data. Administrative controls include stage templates, delegated approvers, country/purpose rules, provider mappings and retention policy with statutory floors non-overridable.

Support **headless only** host integration: every screen calls the conceptual published public contract, not private tables; independent manual case execution remains available if no optional presentation package is installed. Responsive keyboard/screen-reader operation, accessible alternatives to CV parsing and timed assessment, multilingual BCP 47 text, locale-aware dates/times, Theme Manager semantic tokens, error/status/uncertainty explanations and privacy-safe notification content are design requirements. The representative test host is `nuxt-layers-test/application-test-template`; no actual screen or tests have been verified.

## Standards-driven operational design — STD-A through STD-F (approved 20 November 2026)

**Scope:** These six requirements operationalise the approved Recruitment contract. Applicable legislation is evaluated by Legal Compliance for jurisdiction, activity and effective date; voluntary design standards are not certifications. See the [standards assessment](../../../reviews/recruitment-standards-assessment.md) for supporting source mapping.

### STD-A — Jurisdiction-aware policy resolution (approved)

**Purpose:** Determine the lawful and authorised operating conditions for each recruitment command without hard-coding a UK rule set or bypassing another capability's authority.

- **Inputs and owners:** Authentication supplies session/assurance context; Identity supplies tenant/employer/principal relations; Authorisation supplies resource/action/delegation decisions; Legal Compliance supplies applicable jurisdiction-, employer-, role-, activity- and effective-date-specific statutory constraints; Privacy supplies lawful-purpose, disclosure, retention and rights constraints; Configuration supplies validated employer preferences, approval routes and allowed provider modes; Clock supplies reliable effective/recorded-time semantics; Finance/workforce establishment owners supply mandatory hiring mandate or allocation evidence where applicable.
- **Resolution sequence:** (1) authenticate caller or validate authorised applicant intake; (2) resolve employer/tenant, resource, role and cross-organisation delegation; (3) classify operation, candidate-data purpose, country/sector and effective date; (4) retrieve applicable legal and privacy policy version, source, validity window and mandatory floors; (5) resolve permitted organisation configuration and precedence without weakening floors; (6) obtain server-side access decision; (7) validate requisition/application/offer state, evidence freshness and actor mandate; (8) record approved or rejected decision with relevant policy identifiers, effective time, reasons and evidence references; (9) atomically commit Recruitment-owned state and outbox when applicable.
- **Output contract:** `evaluateRecruitmentAction` is a *conceptual* capability-local decision step, not an independent legal compliance verdict. Commands expose allowed, denied, requires-evidence or indeterminate/temporarily-blocked status, permissible next actions, source-qualified reasons, policy/decision revision and secure correlation; external consumers receive redacted details appropriate to their permissions.
- **Non-negotiable:** Unknown or stale **mandatory** permission, finance mandate, safeguarding/check eligibility, or statutory precondition must not be treated as approval. Optional provider failure must not stop unrelated native actions. No single `allowed` flag substitutes for the provenance of legal, privacy, access and business authority. Policy changes require attributable re-evaluation of affected pending cases, not retroactive rewriting of committed history.

### STD-B — Structured, validated selection methods (approved)

- **Selection plan:** For each opening/role and selection campaign, retain a versioned, employer-approved set of essential and desirable job-related criteria, evidence requirements, assessment methods, structured interview rubrics, scoring weights where permitted, interpretation and review/recusal rules, and publishing/disclosure boundaries. Job owns the underlying position specification; Assessment owns its assessment instruments and source results where used; Recruitment owns admission and relevance of those facts to its hiring decision.
- **Method assurance:** Link each scored method to validation/reliability and appropriateness evidence, assessment population/limitations, source and revision, qualified evaluator requirements and any risk/fairness review. Unsupported or stale evidence yields review-required/indeterminate status; no fabricated verified outcome or universal machine score.
- **Execution:** Interviewers and selection panels receive the minimum allowed facts; conflicts of interest, recusals, overrides and evaluation revisions are attributable. Accessibility adjustments are handled in restricted coordination records, not copied into assessor scoring by default. Reasons for recommendation, selection, rejection, appeal and correction remain reviewable to authorised persons.
- **Optional automation:** AI/ML scoring is not a native prerequisite; activate only via explicit configured and legally evaluated workflow, retaining model/rule version, validation evidence, source provenance, appropriate human intervention, subgroup fairness and challenge arrangements. Human-controlled decisions are the default, not a claim of universal legal prohibition on automated significant decisions.

### STD-C — Candidate experience and rights (approved)

- **Public/candidate interfaces:** Accessible vacancy discovery, clear eligibility and pay disclosures when applicable, application and document-submission alternatives, understandable privacy notices, confirmation receipts, progress and outstanding-action queries, request-for-adjustment channels, communication preferences, correction/withdrawal requests, legally applicable challenge/appeal and rights-management status. Candidate-visible status must not expose confidential panel notes, other applicants or restricted protected-characteristic evidence.
- **Service qualities:** WCAG 2.2 AA as governing baseline; keyboard/screen-reader operation, error recovery, reasonable alternatives to CV parsing or timed assessments, localisation via BCP 47/CLDR/ECMA-402, accessible time-zone-aware interview scheduling, and transparent pending/unknown messaging.
- **Case semantics:** Candidate submission acknowledgement is not acceptance to an opening; request to withdraw or erase is not automatically completed; an appealed decision remains historically attributable and may be corrected by a new recorded revision. The accountable controller and relevant data custodian determine lawful rights dispositions. Record notice revision, delivery intent/acknowledgement distinctly and a secure rights-response outcome.

### STD-D — Evidence, retention and records governance (approved)

- **Evidence metadata:** Purpose, owner/controller and authorised recipients, candidate/application and employer context, source/issuer and evidence revision, lawful basis and applicable special-category condition where required, sensitivity/classification, region/transfer restrictions, collected/effective dates, permitted use, retention trigger and expiry, legal holds, correction/supersession and disposition evidence.
- **Lifecycle:** Collect the minimum necessary → validate provenance and access → restricted use and attributable changes → periodic retention/recheck review → lawful archive, return, deletion, anonymisation or hold → recorded disposition and downstream deletion/export notifications as appropriate. Holds suspend incompatible deletion but do not imply unrestricted reading. Legal Compliance/Privacy determine applicable constraints; Recruitment owns its domain case and attachment references; Storage/other custodians own their bytes and independent legal obligations.
- **Contract impacts:** Extend restricted `R-IN-03`/`R-IN-05`/`R-IN-09` evidence and rights intake; `R-OUT-03`/`R-OUT-07` permitted case history, export and disposition; `R-OUT-08` minimised correction/retention events. Record unknown downstream deletion outcomes and reconciliation. No permanent shared cross-employer dossier or automatic transfer of raw credentials into Employment.

### STD-E — Standardised external interchange (approved)

- **Adapter profiles:** Evaluate approved HR Open Standards interchange specifications, schema.org/JobPosting for eligible public vacancies, machine-readable OpenAPI/JSON Schema when HTTP is the chosen public protocol, and Verification/Qualification's appropriate W3C VC/OpenID protocols where qualified claims are exchanged. These are mapping or disclosure formats, **not** Recruitment's canonical persistent state or compulsory transports.
- **Inbound/outbound contracts:** Provider-specific ATS/board identifiers and field mappings are translated to versioned `R-IN-03`/`R-IN-07` and `R-OUT-02`/`R-OUT-09` semantics with schema validation, consent/purpose checks, tenant/agency mapping, timestamp/source provenance, deduplication, revocation and corrections. Respect the REC-13 explicit single authority per fact/operation; remote `accepted`, `published`, `selected`, `delivered` and `confirmed` have separate business meanings.
- **Lifecycle and outages:** Include provider onboarding and scope approval, credentials and signed webhook validation, rate/size limits, backoff, dead-letter or reconciliation queue, version compatibility, export/portability and decommissioning. Unknown or contradictory remote state is an explicit state and cannot silently mutate approved openings or Employment contracts. Native operation remains available without adapters.

### STD-F — Standards traceability and conceptual acceptance (approved)

- **Traceability requirement:** Maintain a per-requirement matrix with: stable requirement identifier; source/edition and legal applicability; normative/voluntary status; effective date and jurisdiction; relevant REC/STD decision; authoritative owner and external data source; inbound/outbound conceptual surface; policy input and validation guard; privacy/disclosure class; required evidence; failure/unknown behaviour; representative acceptance scenario; verification owner and evidence state.
- **Versioning:** Track change of standards, national law and policy separately; Legal Compliance determines applicability, Configuration holds permissible preferences, Recruitment stores applicable decision reference. Revocation, newly applicable requirements and migration create attributable rechecks and amended acceptance evidence, not unlogged mass mutation.
- **Core acceptance scenarios:** (A) stale/missing statutory policy blocks consequential action while valid native work continues; (B) unsupported assessment method requires human review with source-qualified evidence; (C) accessible alternative application and correction/appeal without disclosure leak; (D) expired retention meets valid legal hold and produces attributable delayed disposition; (E) external board ACK is pending until publication proven and duplicate webhook is harmless; (F) an updated regulatory profile changes future decisions but historical cases preserve effective version and lawful review path.
- **Delivery gate:** Future `CONTRACT.md` and host acceptance must demonstrate these obligations with provider-neutral, independently consumable contracts, bounded host composition and capability-owned outbox. A reviewed blueprint is not evidence of released implementation, legal compliance or an ISO certificate.
