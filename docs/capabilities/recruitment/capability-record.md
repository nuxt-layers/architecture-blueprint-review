## Purpose
Potential independently governed **recruitment requisition/vacancy and applicant selection lifecycle**, consuming a versioned Job position definition when applicable, while keeping candidate evidence, application status, interview/assessment findings, employer selection outcomes, offer handover and vacancy publication separate from Job and Employment. Recruitment is a candidate Domain because applications, cases, confidentiality, dispute/appeal and vacancy state may need their own authoritative lifecycles.

## Classification
**Owner-approved conceptual Domain classification under REC-01 (20 November 2026); independently published implementation contract and acceptance not yet verified.** May compose Process/Workflow/Task/Scheduler for execution, but none of these automatically owns applications or selection records. Native business authority and host integration remain for a dedicated review.

## Dependencies
**Candidate cooperation:** Job (optional, frozen position ID/revision and requirements), Identity/Authentication/Authorisation (server-side subject, tenant, permissions), Clock, Employment (dated authoritative engagement/assignment outcomes for vacancy reconciliation), Qualification/Verification (purpose-limited checked evidence), Configuration and Legal Compliance (valid policy/regulatory duties). ## Discovery notes and outstanding findings — 20 November 2026

**Architecture:** **Job defines the position; Recruitment governs whether/when to seek and select someone; Employment governs an actual engagement.** A Job definition, a funded or approved requisition, an advertisement, a submitted application, a selection, an offer and an activated engagement are distinct events/facts, with independent owners and effective times. Do not collapse these into a `filled` boolean.

**Vacancy record questions:** one position might have multiple positions/openings; several incumbents may share assignments; internal replacements may be authorised without public adverts; a posting may represent several approved openings; one opening may appear across many boards. Determine whether the authoritative vacancy entity is a requisition, authorised opening, seat/establishment record, or several linked objects. Never equate Job's position approval or Employment's observed absence with hiring budget permission.

**Publication:** Recruitment is the proposed author of intent/approved vacancy disclosure, Publisher owns actual document, external boards own their remote listing status; Integrations coordinates provider transport. Record requested, acknowledged, confirmed, rejected, uncertain and withdrawn per channel; external confirmation requires evidence. Restrict PII and sensitive pay/role metadata, validate applicable disclosure requirements.

**Financial authority:** Recruitment records the approval source and scope for an opening/funding, but the actual budget/finance decision belongs to the identified accountable owner; neither Job editing permission nor Workflow approval action itself establishes funding. Resolve hiring-manager, HR, finance, delegated multi-party and cross-organisation authority variations.

**Recruitment-to-Employment handover:** a selection is not an accepted offer, an accepted offer is not necessarily an active employment contract, and joining does not prove right-to-work clearance or physical attendance. Define authoritative ownership of pre-engagement offer/acceptance evidence, Employment contract conditions and conditional prerequisites. Use idempotent, purpose-limited public handover with results and reconciliation; do not move candidate dossiers into Employment by default.

**Candidate evidence/security:** define lawful basis/consent when applicable, applicant access, purpose limitation, sensitive data separation, retention/deletion, adverse decisions, fairness/human review, accommodation, appeal, source provenance, automated decision safeguards, agency applicants and cross-border processing. Qualification evidence comparison is not a hiring verdict. Applicable requirements demand qualified Legal Compliance review.

**Operating without other domains:** Recruitment may handle speculative applications/talent pools without a Job position or approved vacancy if rules permit; it must distinguish those cases from authorised vacancies. Employment remains independently usable without Recruitment; Job remains independently usable without either.

**GUI (future review):** recruitment requisition/vacancy dashboard, approval and funding-evidence queue, listing status by channel, candidate/application pipeline, interview/evidence review, selection decision, offer/handover/reconciliation and compliance/retention controls; optional accessible candidate portal. Host owns navigation; headless API, WCAG 2.2 AA, BCP 47, CLDR, Theme Manager. This is a requirements seed, not approved screens.

**Later idealised public input/output contract:** define who supplies position references, applicant applications, consent, verification/qualification evidence, authorised opening requests/funding decisions, interview outcomes, external posting receipts, hire approval and accepted contractual handover; define authoritative outputs for approved vacancy/requisition, listing statuses, application and decision statuses, candidate notices, handover receipts and committed events. Specify tenant/actor/assurance/policy/revision/provenance, idempotency, time/freshness, conflict/unknown/failure, privacy and public/restricted disclosure. None is presently a published API.

**Review questions needing genuine determination:** whether Recruitment should own all vacancies including internal non-recruitment workforce establishment; funding authority boundary; initial sourcing without vacancy; candidate identity and duplicate matching; offer versus Employment agreement owner; agency/federated recruitment; employer-delegated selection rights; appeals and algorithmic decisions; archival/erasure; cross-capability contract direction and standalone minimum acceptance. Reconcile all neighbouring review records during the dedicated Recruitment PR.

**Do not prematurely mark Recruitment as reviewed/approved.** JOB-01–09 approvals settle Job's conceptual boundary and preferred Recruitment classification direction; they do not substitute for Recruitment's full mandated protocol or independent conceptual sign-off.

### Independently meaningful responsibility, records and exclusions

**Proposed classification: Domain capability.** Recruitment is the employer/authorised recruiting organisation's bounded, policy-governed selection and vacancy management domain; it is not a generic job-definition editor, a workforce establishment ledger or a general-purpose workflow engine.

Recruitment **owns**: requisition case and explicit approved opening allocation (authority source and capacity only to extent approved); recruiting campaign and per-channel publication intent/status evidence; sourcing lead and applicant submissions; recruitment-specific candidate dossier and purpose-restricted evidence references; candidacy/application relationship, stage history and evaluation decisions; interview/assessment requests and recorded recruitment evaluations; conflicts, recusals, review and appeals; selection and pre-engagement offer/acceptance negotiation record as a **proposed boundary**; handover request/acknowledgement and reconciliation; recruitment-specific withdrawal, retention holds and defensible history. Records carry employer/tenant, jurisdiction/purpose, revision, source, actor/decision mandate, effective/recorded times and disclosure classification.

**Not owned:** Job position definition; authorised finance budget, payroll, establishment-wide workforce capacity, Employment's actual contracts/engagements/assignments; Identity principals, Profile's canonical personal data; Qualification awards or Verification attestations; assessment instrument's source grades; provider job-board listing as a remote fact; Publisher document artefacts; delivery of messages; workflow/task/scheduler engines; Audit evidence ledger; Reporting aggregates. A candidate record must not silently become a global person/profile identity; an application must never produce an Identity membership or Employment engagement automatically.

**Distinct objects.** A position describes reusable work; an approved requisition represents permission to recruit for a stated purpose, employer and bounded number of openings; an opening allocation is one authorised recruitment capacity unit (not necessarily a formal establishment seat); a campaign can market several openings and a single opening can have several channel adverts. A candidate may submit multiple separately governed applications. A selection decision is not an offer; an offered arrangement is not accepted; acceptance is not an active contract or actual occupancy. Retain independent states and identifiers.

**Operating modes.** Native/manual mode supports authorised internal requisitions, direct applications, manually recorded interviews, private selection, correspondence records and manual Employment handover without Job, Publisher, external ATS, boards or automated workflow. Applications without a vacancy/position are expressly designated speculative or pooled, with separate lawful purpose, expiry and no promise of an opening. An unauthorised requisition cannot be published or represented as approved. Optional delegated/agency, internal-only and external-ATS modes must preserve the owner of each fact, source status and migration/reconciliation history; withdrawal of a provider must not erase legally retained decisions.

### Native state machine and recovery

- **Requisition:** draft → awaiting delegated approval → approved/rejected/cancelled; approved → open/suspended/expired/closed, optionally reopened by a fresh documented authorisation. An expired or revoked authorisation prevents new allocations/publication, without erasing past applications.
- **Opening:** authorised available → reserved for proposed selection → committed to handover → independently confirmed engagement or released/reopened/expired. Reservation does not imply paid headcount, budget approval or employment.
- **Campaign/adverts:** draft → approved disclosure → publication requested → acknowledged/confirmed/rejected/unknown; confirmed → revision requested/withdrawal requested → confirmed withdrawn/unknown. Track each channel separately; no optimistic public visibility assertion on ACK.
- **Application:** received → eligibility/completeness triage → under review → interview/assessment/selection → selected or rejected/withdrawn/closed. Correction, challenge/appeal, restricted hold and re-evaluation are attributable transitions, not erasures.
- **Offer:** drafted → authorised → presented → candidate accepted/declined/expired/withdrawn; contingencies pending/satisfied/failed/unknown; handover requested → Employment acknowledged/rejected/unknown → engagement confirmed, where independently evidenced. Do not infer right-to-work or joining from acceptance.
- **Failures:** duplicate submissions return the original result when key and intent match; contradictory replay rejects; concurrent revisions conflict; source truth disagreement records a disputed state and reconciliation action; timeouts mark unknown/pending, never success; privileged overrides retain reason, approval and audit reference.

**Commands**, conceptually: initiate/revise/approve/cancel requisition; allocate/release/reopen an opening; draft/approve/publish/withdraw a campaign; receive/correct/withdraw application; record evidence, evaluation, conflicts or human selection; issue/revise/withdraw/accept an offer; request/reconcile Employment handover; request/resolve appeal; perform retention/disclosure actions. Server validates actor, employer, mandate, policy, revision, permitted transitions and idempotency. Asynchronous completion has explicit accepted/pending/completed/failed/indeterminate results.

**Queries:** position-linked openings; vacancy/campaign status by channel; authorised applicant/case/pipeline/history; permissible evidence completeness and provenance; offer/appeal/handover state; policy/retention exposure. All reads are tenant- and role-filtered, explicit about stale or incomplete external evidence, with as-of/effective/recorded time and version. Search indexes are derived projections, not recruitment authority.

**Events:** emitted only after durable domain transaction via Recruitment-owned outbox (ADR-0012). Message Queue transports, replays and may duplicate; consumers de-duplicate and independently authorise any subsequent command. A received Recruitment event cannot create an Employment contract, publish a Publisher document, grant access, verify qualification or authorise payment. No distributed exactly-once claim.

**Shared context and invariants:** per-operation employer/tenant and scoped candidate/application/opening reference, actor/agency delegation and session assurance where material, access decision, source/evidence revision, legal/policy/retention version, reason and decision mandate, effective/observed/committed time via Clock, correlation and idempotency where appropriate. These fields are proportionate per operation; **no universal request object**. Stronger controls govern bulk export, protected characteristics, disciplinary data, exceptional overrides and selection decisions. Never overwrite historical decisions on later policy change; recompute eligibility as a new attributable determination. A missing upstream fact remains unknown, not false or approved.

### Legal, security, privacy and provider neutrality

Apply governing OWASP ASVS 5.0 Level 2 with Level 3 safeguards where heightened risk warrants, WCAG 2.2 AA, BCP 47/CLDR, tenant isolation and least privilege. Recruitment handles unusually sensitive candidate information; use purpose-limited collection, encryption/access segregation, retention and deletion schedules, evidence provenance, incident response and restricted bulk exports. Treat special-category and protected-characteristic information as separately controlled; do not make irrelevant protected data visible to assessors or use it in ranking. Accessible alternate application routes, reasonable adjustments and human review/appeal are required where relevant; automated scoring cannot silently deliver solely automated consequential decisions without applicable lawful assessment. Bias monitoring uses lawful minimised measures, not an uncontrolled global candidate profile.

Legal Compliance must identify country/service-specific employment, equality, anti-discrimination, right-to-work, background-check, agency, record-retention, immigration and automated decision requirements; **this proposal is not legal advice or compliance certification**. Candidate notices, withdrawal, correction, subject-access and appeals must identify actual owner and lawful basis; consent is not the default lawful basis for all recruiting processing. Cross-border transfers and agency sharing demand independent permissions and documented purpose. A later legal hold can suspend erasure without making old evidence generally readable.

Native mode uses internal case records and manual publication/communication. Optional provider connectors disclose configured mappings, credential ownership, webhook authenticity, delivery limits, costs, retention/access and cross-border implications. With an external ATS as preferred operational system, explicitly designate local authoritative decisions versus mirrored external facts; import/replay, reconciliation, exit and portability cannot convert provider acknowledgements into local approvals. Preserve limited readable export when a provider is lost, subject to legal restrictions.

### Conceptual GUI, administrator and headless requirements

Host-owned navigation follows **sidebar → Recruitment dashboard**, with role-aware workspaces: requisitions/opening authorisation and funding reference, campaigns and channel publication reconciliation, candidate intake and privacy notices, redacted application pipeline, interview/assessment scheduling, evaluator conflict declarations, guarded selection/offer action with explicit authority, Employment handover and unknown/retry queue, appeals/correction/retention oversight, and restricted operator diagnostics. An optional candidate portal shows submitted applications, document requests, candidate-visible stages and lawful rights; **never** internal evaluation or other candidate data. Administrative controls include stage templates, delegated approvers, country/purpose rules, provider mappings and retention policy with statutory floors non-overridable.

Support **headless only** host integration: every screen calls the conceptual published public contract, not private tables; independent manual case execution remains available if no optional presentation package is installed. Responsive keyboard/screen-reader operation, accessible alternatives to CV parsing and timed assessment, multilingual BCP 47 text, locale-aware dates/times, Theme Manager semantic tokens, error/status/uncertainty explanations and privacy-safe notification content are design requirements. The representative test host is `nuxt-layers-test/application-test-template`; no actual screen or tests have been verified.
